Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Go to Settings → Restore Purchases and tap it. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Reset Data. This permanently deletes all your local data (scores, journal entries, streaks). This action cannot be undone.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Mentra subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel:

  • Open iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions
  • Find Mentra and tap Cancel Subscription

The AI Coach isn't responding

Check your internet connection. The AI Coach requires an active connection to Google's Gemini API. Free users have a limit of 10 messages per day — upgrade to Mentra Pro for unlimited coaching.

How does the AI Coach work?

Your messages are sent securely to Google Gemini to generate responses. Conversations are stored only on your device and never on our servers. You can clear your history anytime from the Coach tab.

Which languages does Mentra support?

Mentra supports: English, Turkish (Türkçe), Arabic (العربية), French (Français), German (Deutsch), and Hindi (हिन्दी). The language follows your device setting, or you can change it in Settings.
