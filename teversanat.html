From Raw Material
to Artistic Language.

Tever Sanat is the cultural division of Tever Forest Products. We exist to support sculptors, curate exceptional wood art, and build a bridge between industrial expertise and creative expression.

For Artists

We provide high-quality veneer and plywood materials to selected artists, enabling them to push the boundaries of their craft.
